India vs Afghanistan: What To Expect

Shreyas Iyer will lead a strong Indian squad featuring established international names including Jasprit Bumrah, Abhishek Sharma, Sanju Samson, Tilak Varma, Axar Patel, Washington Sundar and Arshdeep Singh. India also have the option of handing teenage batting sensation Vaibhav Sooryavanshi a role in the playing XI.

Afghanistan, meanwhile, will be captained by Darwish Rasooli. This is not the same Afghanistan side India recently faced in the bilateral T20I series, with several prominent names absent from the Asian Games squad. The Afghan side will therefore present a different challenge, particularly if the surface offers assistance to their spinners.

India's preparation has already been disrupted by rain. Their only recent match practice came in a rain-reduced five-over game against Japan, which India won by two runs.

Nisshin Weather Forecast for September 28

Weather could once again become the biggest talking point. The India Meteorological Department forecast for Nisshin puts Monday's temperature at around 21.4°C minimum and 29.9°C maximum, with partly cloudy conditions and the possibility of light rain or a thunderstorm.

Other forecasts are considerably wetter, with AccuWeather indicating a 91% chance of rain and 22% chance of thunderstorms during Monday daytime.

What If India vs Afghanistan Is Washed Out?

If rain interrupts play but enough cricket is possible, officials can reduce the number of overs and use the Duckworth-Lewis-Stern (DLS) method to determine a revised target.

However, if the quarter-final is completely abandoned without a result, India will progress to the semi-finals because they are the higher-seeded team due to their superior ICC T20I ranking. The tournament regulations use the higher-ranked/seeded side as the basis for advancement in such a knockout washout scenario.
